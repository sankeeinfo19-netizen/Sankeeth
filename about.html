About Me

Nine years of turning brand ideas into things people can actually use

I'm a Senior Visualiser based in Chennai, working across branding, UI/UX and motion — from first sketch to the guideline a team can build on for years.

My Story

From production artist to creative direction

I started out doing hands-on production — cutting video, building layouts, shipping logos on tight deadlines. That production instinct never left; it's why I still care about whether a guideline survives contact with a real campaign, or whether a Figma file actually holds up once a developer opens it.

Over nine years and four roles, I've moved from executing briefs to owning them: leading design teams, mentoring junior designers, and setting brand strategy end to end. What hasn't changed is the throughline across every medium — a consistent visual language that helps a brand communicate clearly and connect with the people it's for.

Today I split my time between motion and post-production, brand and visual identity systems, and UI/UX for web and mobile — usually all three on the same project, because that's how real briefs actually arrive.
